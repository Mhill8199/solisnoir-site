Privacy Policy

ENTITY: SOLIS NOIR LLC (HAMILTON, MT) APPLICATIONS: THE BITE & AFFILIATED APPS EFFECTIVE: NOVEMBER 20, 2026

1. Overview & Scope

Solis Noir LLC ("Solis Noir," "we," "our," or "us"), headquartered in Hamilton, Montana, operates the solisnoir.com website and publishes mobile and web applications, including The Bite offshore marine telemetry and angling companion app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard information when you visit our website or use our mobile applications.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Information: When you create an account or subscribe to paid features, we collect your email address, authentication identifiers, and subscription status. Payment processing is handled securely by Apple App Store, Google Play, or Stripe; we do not store full payment card numbers.
  • Location & Marine Telemetry Data: With your explicit device permission, The Bite accesses foreground and/or background GPS location data to display nearby NOAA buoy stations, sea surface temperature (SST) charts, tide stations, and offline marine waypoints.
  • User-Generated Logs & Waypoints: Catch logs (species, size, general fishing zone, notes) and saved private waypoints you enter into The Bite are stored securely in your private cloud account (powered by Supabase) and cached locally on your device for offline offshore access.
  • Website Inquiries: Information you voluntarily submit through our website contact form (name, email address, inquiry category, and message) is transmitted via Netlify Forms solely to respond to your request.
  • Diagnostics & Device Telemetry: Basic crash logs, app version, and device OS metadata used strictly to maintain app stability and performance.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information strictly to:

  • Deliver real-time oceanographic buoy telemetry, satellite SST overlays, tide schedules, and personalized "My Bite" summaries.
  • Synchronize your private catch logs, custom waypoints, and offline map caches across sessions.
  • Verify active annual or recurring subscriptions and restore purchases across devices.
  • Respond to customer support, partnership, and press inquiries.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or private fishing waypoints to third parties or data brokers.

4. Data Storage, Security & Retention

Application data is encrypted in transit via TLS and stored at rest using enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure (Supabase and Netlify). Private waypoints and catch records remain strictly isolated to your authenticated user account unless you explicitly choose to share a summary.

We retain your account data for as long as your account remains active. You may request complete deletion of your account and associated cloud records at any time.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Account Deletion

Depending on your jurisdiction (including California, Montana, and other U.S. states), you have the right to access, export, correct, or permanently delete personal data we hold about you.

To delete your account in-app or submit a privacy or data-deletion request, contact us at hello@solisnoir.com with the subject line "Privacy / Data Request". We verify and complete deletion requests within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Our website and applications are not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
